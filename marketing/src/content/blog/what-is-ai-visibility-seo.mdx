Search is changing. Google AI Mode, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and other AI-powered engines are reshaping how users discover information. If your SEO strategy only accounts for traditional blue links, you are missing a growing share of search traffic.

## What is AI visibility?

AI visibility refers to whether your brand, content, or website appears in AI-generated search results. When a user asks Google AI Mode or ChatGPT a question, these systems synthesize answers from multiple sources and cite them. Your AI visibility is whether your domain gets cited in those answers.

This is fundamentally different from traditional SEO rankings. You are not competing for position 1 through 10 on a results page. You are competing to be one of the sources that the AI references in its generated answer.

## Why does it matter?

The data shows that a significant and growing percentage of searches now trigger AI-generated answers. For informational queries, AI answers often appear above traditional results. Users who get their answer from the AI-generated response may never scroll to the organic results.

If your competitors are being cited and you are not, you are losing visibility regardless of your traditional rankings.

## How to track AI visibility

Tracking AI visibility requires checking whether your domain appears in AI-generated answers for your target keywords. This means querying multiple AI search engines and analyzing whether your content is cited.

DataWise's [AI Visibility tools](/features/ai-visibility) automate this process. You can check your presence in Google AI Mode, ChatGPT, and Perplexity responses for any keyword.

## Getting started

The first step is understanding where you stand today. Run your top keywords through an AI visibility check and see which ones cite your domain. From there, you can develop a strategy to improve your citation rate.
